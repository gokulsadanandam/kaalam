Kaalam · காலம்

Rasi Palan and Panchangam for every day

A Tamil daily calendar for Android, in Tamil and English.

  • Daily Rasi Palan for all 12 rasis, worked out from the Moon's transit and the day's Panchangam.
  • Tithi, nakshatram, yogam, karanam, Tamil date, sunrise and sunset.
  • Rahu Kalam, Yamagandam, Kuligai, Nalla Neram and Gowri Panchangam.
  • A home-screen widget and optional reminders.
  • No sign-in, no ads, no tracking.
