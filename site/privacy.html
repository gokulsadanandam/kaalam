Kaalam · காலம்

Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Kaalam (காலம்) is an Android app that shows the daily Tamil Rasi Palan, Panchangam and auspicious timings. It is made by Gokul Sadanandam, an individual developer in India (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information Kaalam handles, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • No sign-in and no account. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics or tracking, and we never sell data.
  • Your settings stay on your phone.
  • If you use “Find my rasi”, your birth date and time are used once to work out your rasi and are not stored.

1. Information the app handles

To show the day's reading

To fetch a day's reading, the app sends our server the rasi you picked, the app language and the date. These aren't linked to you or your device.

“Find my rasi” (optional)

If you don't know your rasi, you can enter your birth date and time. The app sends them to our server over an encrypted connection, the server works out your rasi, nakshatra and pada, and sends the answer back. We don't save your birth details in any database.

Technical information

Our hosting providers keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, request addresses and request times, for security and troubleshooting. These logs are kept for a limited time and are not used to identify you.

What we don't collect

Kaalam doesn't access your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera. It doesn't use an advertising ID, third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

2. What stays on your phone

  • Your chosen rasi and language, and your widget and reminder settings.
  • The latest reading, so the widget works when you're offline.

This is kept in the app's private storage, isn't backed up to the cloud, and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Permissions

  • Internet, to download the day's reading.
  • Notifications, alarms and starting after a restart, only for the reminders and the lock-screen summary you turn on (for example, 5 minutes before Rahu Kalam). They are scheduled on your phone.

4. Who handles information

  • Supabase hosts our server, which works out the readings. It handles requests only on our behalf.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website.
  • Google Play distributes the app. Google's privacy policy covers your use of Google Play.

We don't share or sell information to anyone else, except where the law requires us to.

5. Children

Kaalam is a general-audience app and isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS).

7. Your rights

We don't keep personal information about you, so there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or concern, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, write to us at the address below. We'll reply within 7 days.

8. About the readings

Rasi Palan readings are for general guidance and cultural interest only. They are not professional, medical, legal or financial advice.
